Timothy Mamo

Platform Specialist, Amsterdam

Timothy Mamo helps growing companies make the most of the cloud, with a focus on Cloud Native technologies and practices. His path into the cloud was a varied one: he studied aerospace engineering and worked in the automotive industry before moving into the world of cloud platforms. He enjoys working with others and helping them improve and understand, at times with some Mediterranean gusto.

Timothy is a member of FikaWorks, a consultancy of senior professionals, collectively owned as a cooperative.

He builds shift2eu in the open and provides it free to use.
